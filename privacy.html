Privacy policy

How Bloom Assistant handles information in its personal Instagram connection testing workspace.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This is a testing service. Connecting an account does not enable automatic posting or live analytics. The current dashboard and reports use sample data, and assistant replies are local templates—not a connected AI model.

1. About Bloom Assistant

Bloom Assistant is a personal testing project for planning Instagram content and trying an official Meta account-connection flow. Its current backend is designed for one workspace owner, not a public multi-user service. The contact for this project and its privacy requests is abc075528@gmail.com.

This policy covers Bloom’s frontend and backend. Meta, the hosting provider, and the email provider handle information under their own policies when you use their services.

2. Information handled

  • Instagram account connection: with your authorization, the backend receives a Meta access token and reads the IDs, usernames, and names of Instagram accounts assigned to that credential. During account selection, it temporarily stores that list. Once you confirm an account, it stores the selected profile, encrypted access token, connection time, and token-expiration time if supplied by Meta.
  • Owner login and security: the backend processes the private Bloom owner password to verify access. The configured password is managed in the hosting environment. The backend stores hashed session identifiers, session-expiration times, and temporary OAuth security state. A necessary session cookie is stored in the browser.
  • Content you plan: titles, captions, formats, planned dates, draft/schedule status, uploaded image previews, and saved idea titles are stored in your browser’s local storage. The current planner does not upload those drafts or images to the backend or Meta. Assistant messages and topic-generated ideas are kept in the current page’s memory; explicitly saved idea titles are stored locally.
  • Technical and support information: the backend uses request IP information for in-memory rate limiting. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request metadata, and logs to operate and secure the service. If you email the contact address, the message and information you include are processed to handle your request.

Bloom does not ask for or store your Instagram or Facebook password. Enter those passwords only on the official Meta sign-in pages. Do not send us passwords, app secrets, access tokens, or login codes.

3. How information is used

Connection data is used to complete your requested Meta login, let you select an assigned account, save and verify that connection, and attempt to revoke access when you disconnect. Security information is used to restrict access to the workspace owner, prevent forged or replayed login callbacks, and limit abusive requests. Browser-local content is used to display your planner and saved ideas.

The current backend does not fetch your activity insights, messages, comments, or advertising records, and does not publish content. A Meta configuration template may request broader permissions than these features use. Review Meta’s consent screen and do not approve access you do not want. Granting a permission does not mean Bloom currently implements the corresponding feature.

No connected AI provider receives your prompts or account data in this version. Reports downloaded from the interface contain sample metrics and local schedule text, not access tokens or uploaded image previews.

4. Sharing and service providers

Bloom does not sell personal information or use account data for targeted advertising. The current application does not include third-party advertising or analytics scripts.

  • Meta: receives requests needed for authorization, assigned-account discovery, connection verification, and revocation. Meta controls its own sign-in and permissions screens.
  • Hosting: the deployed backend uses Render to run the service and hold its server-side data and private environment settings. The separate GitHub Pages demo is a static website hosted by GitHub; its drafts are local to that browser origin.
  • Email: privacy and support requests sent to the published Gmail address are processed by the email provider and the project contact.

Providers may process information in locations outside your country under their own terms and policies. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Access tokens and pending account-selection data are encrypted on the backend using AES-256-GCM, with the encryption key held separately in the hosting environment. Tokens are not returned to the browser. Production owner-session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. These protections reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security.

  • Owner sessions: stop being valid after eight hours by default, or after 30 days if you choose “Remember this device.” Locking owner settings logs out this device and invalidates its session. Clearing the cookie or losing temporary server storage also prevents continued use of that sign-in. Expired session records are cleaned when a new owner session is created.
  • OAuth and pending account selection: are valid for ten minutes. Expiry blocks use; it does not necessarily immediately erase the encrypted record. Starting a new login, completing selection, logging out, disconnecting, or session cleanup removes or replaces the relevant stored information.
  • Saved connection: remains until disconnected, replaced, deleted in response to a verified request, or lost with the server’s storage. Token expiration blocks use but does not automatically erase the saved record.
  • Free testing storage: is temporary. Server sessions and connections may be lost after sleep, restart, or deployment. Environment settings are managed separately by the host. Losing a token locally does not revoke Meta’s authorization.
  • Optional persistent hosting: can retain server records across restarts. If an operator creates backups, those copies require separate retention and deletion handling; deletion from the live database does not promise instant erasure from storage remnants or provider-managed systems.
  • Browser drafts and saved ideas: remain until removed in the app or through your browser’s site-data controls. Data is origin-specific, so the GitHub Pages demo and the backend-hosted workspace have separate browser storage.
  • Support email and infrastructure logs: may be retained to handle requests, security needs, or legal obligations, subject to the operator’s and providers’ retention practices.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can choose not to connect Instagram, decline Meta permissions, lock the owner session, disconnect the selected account, or remove the integration in Meta’s account settings. Disconnect attempts remote revocation and deletes the saved local credential even if Meta is unavailable; if revocation fails, remove the integration through Meta as well. Logging out alone does not disconnect the account.

For browser-local data, remove drafts or saved ideas in the interface or clear site data in your browser. The backend cannot remotely clear another browser’s local storage or downloaded reports.

See the data-deletion instructions for the full process. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information handled by the project by contacting abc075528@gmail.com. Include only the information needed to identify your account and request. We may need to verify that you own or are authorized to manage the account, without asking for passwords or access tokens. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements; deletion is not instant or automatic on receipt of an email.
