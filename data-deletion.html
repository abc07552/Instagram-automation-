Data-deletion instructions

How to remove a saved connection, clear local content, and contact the project about your data.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This is an instructions page, not an automatic deletion callback. Visiting it does not delete anything. Email requests are handled manually after any necessary ownership verification.

1. Disconnect Instagram from Bloom

  1. Open the deployed backend’s copy of Bloom and open the account menu.
  2. Unlock the connection settings using the private Bloom owner password, not your Instagram password.
  3. Select Disconnect for the saved Instagram account.

Disconnect removes the saved connection record, including its encrypted token, selected profile details, and connection timestamps, from Bloom’s live database. It also clears pending account-selection data. The backend attempts to revoke access through Meta.

If Meta revocation fails, Bloom still deletes its local credential and tells you to revoke the integration through Meta. Clearing local storage or losing a token on a free-hosting restart does not revoke Meta’s authorization.

2. Revoke the integration through Meta

In the Meta account used to authorize Bloom, find the connected apps or business integrations settings and remove the relevant Bloom app. Menu names vary by account and login method; follow Meta’s displayed removal instructions.

You can do this even if Bloom is unavailable or its free-testing server has lost the saved connection. Revocation controls future access; it does not automatically clear browser-local drafts, downloaded files, or all existing server records.

3. Remove browser-local content

Delete drafts/posts from the planner and unsave ideas in the interface. To clear all locally saved content, use your browser’s site-data controls for the Bloom site you used. This may include draft titles, captions, image previews, dates, content types, draft status, and saved idea titles.

If you used both GitHub Pages and the backend-hosted site, each origin has separate local storage. Clear the relevant site data for both. Close the workspace tab to discard assistant messages held in page memory. Delete any downloaded reports from your own device separately.

Clearing browser data alone does not remove the server connection. Logging out deletes the current owner session but does not disconnect Instagram.

4. Request help with access or deletion

Email abc075528@gmail.com with the subject Bloom data deletion request.

Include:

  • The Instagram username associated with the request.
  • The Bloom website address you used.
  • What you want removed, such as a saved connection or information in a support email.

Only provide information needed to identify the request. Do not send passwords, app secrets, access tokens, login codes, or payment information. We may need to verify that you own or are authorized to manage the account. An email request does not trigger immediate automatic deletion.

Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements. We cannot remotely clear another browser’s local storage, erase files downloaded to your device, or delete records controlled independently by Meta, hosting, or email providers. Data retained in provider-managed systems or any separately maintained backups may require separate handling.

5. Free testing and retention

The free testing backend uses temporary storage. Account connections and sessions can disappear after sleep, restart, or deployment; do not rely on it for durable records or as proof of remote revocation. Environment settings, provider records, and browser data are separate from that temporary database.

For security, retention, service providers, and the information currently handled by the app, read the privacy policy. These pages do not certify Meta App Review approval or enable automatic posting.
